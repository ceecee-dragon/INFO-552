2025

Turning a Library into a Gallerywalk

Little bit of background. The library space used to be a restroom. And then someone started using it for storage. My principal told me it was the library. The first day I saw it, we couldn't enter because it was floor to ceiling boxes and tables. Floor to ceiling, boxes and tables. It took months to weed the collection and then triage the collection after the leftover plumbing from when it was a bathroom burst. The leftover plumbing burst twice. I needed help from a professional archivist to figure out how to preserve and protect what remained. I'm still working in this place. It's still very much a work in progress.
