Mission Statement

Femme Fatale is a digital sanctuary for femme-identifying individuals to share, learn, and reclaim their narratives. We are a collective platform that affirms identity, empowers with knowledge, and celebrates the complexity of the female experience.

Featured Story

"Tale from a Grave: Bonds"

I spy with my little eyes something brown. Dirt. I feel with my tiny fingertips something cold. Dirt. I have a surprise for you. Something you always wanted. I have an urge? A desire? A chance to save your sister. I have a sister. For the first time in a long time I can feel something other than the cold dirt around me. The warm threads of fate are unfurling all around me, forming bonds, leading me to a place somewhere outside this coffin that woman left me in. A sister. I have a sister. A sister, a friend, a playmate. Someone to protect, someone to save or help into a grave.
