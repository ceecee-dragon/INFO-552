About Femme Fatale

Femme Fatale is more than a website — it's a sanctuary for storytelling, learning, and liberation. Founded by CeeCee and her commitment to feminism and community healing, Femme Fatale reclaims space in a world that often tries to marginalize women, police women, or define what being a woman is.

About CeeCee

CeeCee is fabulosity incarnate. I don't know what else to say. Through this platform, she channels care, courage, and creativity to build a space where no story is too small, no truth too dangerous.
