About

Hi! I’m CeeCee. I’m a South Philly native, which means I got a whole lot to say about everything. I am sometimes obnoxiously loud even though I'm definitely an introvert. I love life. I bring that same passion and ethos to the library every day—along with a lot of books, playlists, and laughs. My students will tell you that I’m good at a lot of things: finding the perfect graphic novel, helping with research, organizing chaos, solving Chromebook drama, and matching readers with just-right stories. But one thing I definitely cannot do? Sing. (One of my first graders walked in on me lip syncing and he threatened to tell his mama.)

—a K–8 school librarian with a background in ESL, English Language Arts, social studies, and creative writing. Right now, I’m also working toward my master’s degree, learning even more about how to support curious minds, build inclusive collections, and keep our library the heart of the school.

 

When I’m not working with students, I’m reading, writing, listening to music, or making art. I have a deep love for:

  • Fairy tales (especially the ones with twists!)
  • Dystopian fiction (Brave New Girl by Rachel Vincent and The House of the Scorpion by Nancy Farmer are my thing)
  • All things zombie (Warm Bodys, Rot & Ruin, and allegedly homegirl Sherrilyn Kenyon is writing YA zombie fics.)
  • Comics and graphic novels (of every kind)
 

My all-time favorite book to read is Maniac Magee by Jerry Spinelli—it never fails to move me. And if you catch me reading aloud, chances are it’s Jazz by Walter Dean Myers, because that rhythm, that language? Pure magic.
